Advance your nursing practice

In today’s changing healthcare landscape, the need for advanced practice nurses who deliver holistic, patient-centered care has never been greater. Nurse practitioners play a vital role in meeting these demands, combining clinical expertise, compassion, and autonomy.

The NP programs at the University of Rochester School of Nursing are built for experienced, working nurses ready to elevate their practice. Our curriculum expands your scope of care, strengthens professional autonomy, and prepares you to make a meaningful impact on patient and community health.

Earning your NP licensure and national certification opens doors to new leadership opportunities, deeper patient relationships, and a more influential role in shaping the future of care.

STUDENT STORY

Meaningful career experiences. Shaped by mentorship. Rooted in Rochester. 

Matthew Drew '24N (MS) found a passion for cardiology during his clinicals as an Adult-Gerontology Acute Care Nurse Practitioner student, with help from his preceptor and advisor, Associate Professor of Clinical Nursing Rebecca Tucker. Now, Drew and Tucker are co-workers on the same unit, giving back to their community as NPs at Strong Memorial Hospital's Heart Center. The two longtime Rochester residents share their journeys into nursing, and what makes this region special to them. 

A proud history. An exciting future.

Dr. Loretta Ford, our founding dean, revolutionized nursing more than 50 years ago by co-founding the nurse practitioner role, laying the foundation for advanced nursing education. Her visionary leadership reshaped the profession, elevating the role of nurses and nurse practitioners while fostering greater respect and recognition for their critical contributions to healthcare. Since then, UR nurses have continued to lead, inspiring and paving the way for generations of nurses to follow.

Today, our nurse practitioner programs are ranked top in the nation, setting the benchmark for excellence in specialties such as gerontology, pediatrics, and mental health. We were also first in the country to prepare advanced practice nurses as NPs in the acute care setting. It is this enduring commitment to innovation and excellence that prepares our graduates to deliver outstanding patient care and advance their careers with confidence.
