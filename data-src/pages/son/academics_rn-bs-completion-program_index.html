The RN to BS program offers an exceptional learning experience for associate's or diploma-prepared nurses who want to earn a bachelor's degree.

Program length

4

semesters of core coursework

Scholarship opportunities

50-100%

scholarships available

Application deadline

July 1

For Fall 2026 program start

Complete the RN to BS program to take the next step in your career.

Whether you want to pursue an opportunity in your workplace, explore a new nursing role, or go on to teach or earn an advanced practice degree — this is a great place to make connections and build upon your experience and clinical skills.

Gain new knowledge about patient care and population health with interactive and hands-on learning experiences. Nursing faculty from the University of Rochester will guide you every step of the way.

Complete 100% of the online coursework using mobile technology in the RN to BS program. An optimized iPad device will contain all reading materials and activities needed for the program.

There’s very good support from the faculty. I struggle with writing and we have writing resources. My instructor is dedicated and always cheerful.

Meet the Program Director

Michael Rosario-McCabe, EdD, RN, CCM

RN to BS Program Director

Michael Rosario-McCabe brings more than 30 years of clinical experience to his role as a faculty member at the School of Nursing. He worked in both the hospital and home care settings, developing a specialty practice in the care of HIV-positive patients before joining the School of Nursing faculty in 2018. At the School of Nursing, he has taught and advised students across the school’s undergraduate programs and became program director in 2022.

Scholarships & grants

We are proud to offer significant scholarship support to RN to BS students. Earn your bachelor's from one of the nation's top nursing schools at a cost comparable to others in the region.

50% Finger Lakes Regional Scholarship

A 50% scholarship for nurses who live or work in the 18-county Western NY region.

55% Affiliate Professional Development Grant

A 55% scholarship for nurses who work at a University of Rochester affiliate organization.

100% SON Tuition Grant

Full tuition coverage for University of Rochester employees who combine employee tuition benefits with the SON Tuition Grant.
