Our ABSN Students

DISCOVER

Where do they come from?

Our accelerated students come to Rochester, New York from across the state, nation, and world to pursue a career in nursing. They bring a wide range of backgrounds and career experiences—from nutrition and neuroscience, to music and marketing. Regardless of their previous fields of study or practice, all share a desire to make a difference in the lives of others.

ABSN Students at a Glance

33%

Students are from groups underrepresented in nursing

On average, one of every three students in the Accelerated Nursing Programs are from groups underrepresented in nursing.

24%

Students are male

On average, nearly one fourth of our students are male, which is twice the national average for men in the nursing profession.

50+

States, plus other countries

Accelerated students have come from across the U.S. and around the world to pursue a new career in nursing.

Where are they now?

Twenty years ago, the University of Rochester began offering an accelerated path for college graduates to pursue a second career in nursing. Since then nearly 4,000 graduates have joined the nursing workforce as highly-skilled clinicians. They join a strong and respected profession working to improve care for patients in communities across Rochester and beyond.
