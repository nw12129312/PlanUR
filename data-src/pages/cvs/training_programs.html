Programs for Trainees

At the Center for Visual Science (CVS), we recognize the value of training beyond formal coursework and provide trainees with enriching opportunities that foster both scientific growth and a strong vision research community.

  • Colloquia & Journal Clubs
    Trainees help organize and actively participate in the Boynton Colloquium Series, hosting prominent vision scientists, coordinating visits, and leading journal clubs on speakers’ work. They also engage in the annual David Knill Lectureship and various department-sponsored seminars.
  • Research Talks & Retreats
    Trainees present annually at CVS Research Talks, an informal forum for sharing work-in- progress and honing presentation skills. They also contribute to CVS Retreats, which combine research talks, poster sessions, strategic planning, and industry networking, offering awards for top student presentations.
  • Symposia & Conferences
    CVS hosts biennial Symposia on cutting-edge topics, with recent themes like Active Vision and Neural Imaging. Trainees benefit from travel fellowships, dedicated trainee activities, and the chance to interact with global leaders in vision science.
  • Journal Clubs, Workshops & Multi-lab Meetings
    Trainees participate in multi-lab journal clubs, topical workshops, and collaborative research meetings, where they lead discussions and present their work.
